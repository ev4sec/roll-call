# Architecture: settled technical decisions

Decisions that have been argued and are not re-opened without new evidence.
`systems-architect` reads this before answering, so a decision recorded here
stops being re-derived.

**Record the reasoning, not just the choice.** A decision without its reasoning
gets reversed by the next person who sees only its cost. Include the option that
was rejected and what it would have bought, because that is what a future
argument will actually be about.
